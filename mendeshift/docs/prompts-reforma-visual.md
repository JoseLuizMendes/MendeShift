# Prompts das imagens do MendeShift

Imagens geradas com o ImageGen integrado. A composição da home deixa a área do texto escura. As capas são mockups conceituais, baseados nas cores e funcionalidades de cada projeto.

## hero

Arquivo `public/portfolio/hero.webp`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: widescreen 2:1 background plate for MendeShift, a software engineer’s black-and-red portfolio. A massive solid sculptural slab of matte graphite in the shape of a fractured geological ridgeline, its broken strata revealing a precise array of physical PCB traces and tiny red signal paths. Shot with dramatic side lighting, real material depth and carefully controlled black levels. Composition: keep the entire left 43% almost-black quiet negative space for a title, keep the recognizable silhouette centered between 62% and 78% horizontally and within the central 72% of the height for responsive crops. The right half has broad graphite highlights, unmistakable tonal differences for a live halftone shader, and restrained coral-red transmission lights. Palette #0b0c0f, warm gray charcoal, #ff4d4f. No people, text, letters, logos, code, watermark, neon haze, particles, UI, or baked-in dithering. A premium creative-coding portfolio background with a bold legible silhouette.
```

## Retrato de O Estúdio

Arquivo public/portfolio/studio-portrait.webp. Usei o retrato anterior como referência de identidade no ImageGen.

```text
Create a new, sharply focused editorial studio portrait for the MendeShift founder section. The reference image is an identity reference for José Luiz Mendes. Preserve his real face, skin tone, dark hair, mustache and subtle facial hair. Replace the old close-up and transparent cutout with a new photograph. Show him seated on a simple black stool, naturally facing slightly off center, wearing a matte black long-sleeve crewneck, both hands relaxed and fully visible together on his lap. Landscape 5:4 composition, framed from just above the head to mid-thigh. BOTH shoulders, elbows, forearms, wrists, and fingers entirely inside the image with generous breathing room on every edge; any crop is below the hands at mid-thigh only. Place him centrally. Plain solid charcoal #0b0c0f seamless backdrop with even tone throughout; no bokeh, blur, fog, texture, gradient halo, vignette, transparency or cutout. Soft directional neutral key light and a subtle deep red rim light from one side, restrained black and red editorial direction. Crisp focus and natural detail across his face, hair, arms and hands; realistic skin and fabric. No props apart from the stool, no text, logo, border, branding or watermark.
```
